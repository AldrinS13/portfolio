Quest log

From the phones to the code

I started on the phones at a BPO in Cebu City in 2014. Workforce management found me by accident, and with it forecasting, real-time monitoring, and reports that told operations what to do next.

Over ten years I moved from analyst to training supervisor for teams in five regions. The part I kept coming back to was the same: finding the one number that explains the rest, then building something so nobody has to find it by hand again.

Friends who write code pointed out that building tools is programming. So I am learning to do it properly.
